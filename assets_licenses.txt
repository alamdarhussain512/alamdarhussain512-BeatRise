Free assets curated for BeatRise-Starter

Art & UI:
- Kenney.nl free asset packs (UI, GUI) - https://kenney.nl/assets
  License: Most Kenney assets are public domain (CC0) or permissive; check specific pack.

Music & Loops:
- Mixkit Music (free, royalty-free) - https://mixkit.co/free-music/
  License: Free for commercial use, no attribution required (check Mixkit license page).
- YouTube Audio Library - https://studio.youtube.com/audiolibrary/music
  License: Free to use, some tracks require attribution; verify per track.

SFX:
- Freesound.org - https://freesound.org/ (filter for CC0 or CC-BY)
  License: Varies; prefer CC0 or provide attribution in credits for CC-BY.
- Zapsplat (free tier) - https://www.zapsplat.com/
  License: Free with attribution for free tier.

Fonts & Icons:
- Google Fonts - https://fonts.google.com/
- Font Awesome (free icons) - https://fontawesome.com/

Notes:
- I will include an assets_licenses.txt file in the project and mark exactly which files require attribution.
- For MVP I will prefer CC0/public-domain assets and Mixkit free music to avoid attribution overhead.
